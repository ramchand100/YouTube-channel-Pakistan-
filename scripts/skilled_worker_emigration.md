## Part 1 — The Anomaly

Pichle financial year, Pakistan ne apni tareekh ka sabse bara remittance number record kiya — $41.6 billion, sirf barah mahinon mein [SOURCE: Business Recorder, 2026]. Remittance yani woh paisa jo bahar mulk mein kaam karne wale Pakistani apne ghar walon ko wapas bhejte hain. Isi saal, ek andaza Pakistan Institute of Development Economics se jura hua bhi saamne aaya. Agar har saal 100,000 skilled professionals — doctors, engineers, accountants jaisay log — mulk chor dein, to remittances milne ke baad bhi Pakistan ko taqreeban $4.2 billion ka salana nuksan hota hai [SOURCE: PIDE, via Profit by Pakistan Today, 2025] [VERIFY: yeh ek scenario-based andaza hai, asal 2025 ke skilled-emigration numbers is se kaafi kam hain — ise aagay chal kar dekhenge]. Toh jab ek government-subsidized doctor ya engineer hamesha ke liye Pakistan chor deta hai, mulk asal mein khota kya hai — aur kya remittances waqai us nuksan ko pura karti hain?

Yeh sawal itna seedha nahi hai jitna lagta hai. Remittances Pakistan ki poori economy ka taqreeban 8 percent banati hain [SOURCE: Gulf Research Center/KAS policy brief, 2025]. Yani jitna paisa poora mulk saal bhar mein kamata hai, uska ek bara hissa bahar se aane wale isi paisay se banta hai. Toh yeh dalil sunne mein sahi lagti hai: "haan, log ja rahay hain, lekin woh paisa bhi bhej rahay hain, toh barabar ho jata hai." Is video mein hum yeh dalil number by number test karenge. Dekhenge ke yeh waqai barabar hoti hai — ya yeh do alag kahaniyan hain jo hum ghalti se ek hi kahani samajh rahay hain.

## Part 2 — The Exit

Pakistan mein har saal jitne log kaam ki talash mein bahar jate hain, unhe register karne ka kaam Bureau of Emigration and Overseas Employment — yani BEOE — karta hai. Yeh idara logon ko skill ke hisaab se categorize karta hai: unskilled, semi-skilled, skilled, highly skilled, aur highly qualified [SOURCE: Gulf Research Center/KAS policy brief, 2025]. Lekin ismein ek badi khaali jagah hai: BEOE sirf unhe register karta hai jo work permit par jate hain. Jo log student visa par jate hain, phir wahin settle ho jate hain, ya jo visit visa par jakar naukri dhoond lete hain — unka koi record nahi hai [SOURCE: Gulf Research Center/KAS policy brief, 2025]. Yani jo numbers hum abhi dekhne wale hain, woh sabse kam andaza hain, sabse zyada nahi.

2025 mein, BEOE ke register ke mutabiq 3,795 doctors, 5,946 engineers, aur 5,659 accountants Pakistan chor kar gaye [SOURCE: TechJuice, 2026]. 2024 mein yeh number tha 3,642 doctors aur 8,018 engineers [SOURCE: TechJuice, 2026]. Do saalon ko jama karein — 2024 aur 2025 — to taqreeban 5,000 doctors, 11,000 engineers, aur 13,000 se zyada accountants Pakistan se ja chukay hain [SOURCE: Tribune India, 2025]. Nurses ka table sabse zyada tez badla hai: 2013 mein sirf 315 nurses bahar gayi thin, 2023 mein yeh number barh kar 4,880 ho gaya [SOURCE: Gulf Research Center/KAS policy brief, 2025]. Yeh isliye bhi ajeeb hai kyunki Pakistan mein pehle se hi nurses ki kami hai — general hospital wards mein aik nurse ke zimme 40 mareez hote hain, jabke Pakistan Nursing Council ki apni sifarish hai ke yeh ratio 3 nurses per 10 mareez hona chahiye [SOURCE: Gulf Research Center/KAS policy brief, 2025].

Lekin yahan asal tasveer thora mukhtalif hai jitni yeh numbers akele dikhate hain. 2023 mein, taqreeban 862,000 log kaam ke liye register hue, aur inmein se 96 percent Gulf gaye — Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar [SOURCE: Gulf Research Center/KAS policy brief, 2025]. Pichle paanch dahaion se, "highly skilled" ya "highly qualified" category ka hissa kabhi bhi total emigrants ka 10 percent se zyada nahi raha [SOURCE: Gulf Research Center/KAS policy brief, 2025]. Doctors, engineers, IT professionals — yeh sab jo "brain drain" ki kahani mein sunte hain — woh Pakistan se jaane walon ka ek chota sa hissa hain. Bari tadaad un logon ki hai jo mazdoori, driving, ya construction ke kaam ke liye Gulf jate hain.

Toh log kyun ja rahay hain? Isi saal ki ek PIDE survey ke mutabiq, Pakistan mein graduate unemployment 16 percent hai, jabke overall unemployment sirf 5 percent hai [SOURCE: PIDE, 2024]. Matlab: jitna zyada padha likha aadmi, utna kam mauka. Isi wajah se, PIDE ke apne survey mein 78 percent logon ne kaha ke woh behtar income ke liye mulk chorna chahte hain [SOURCE: PIDE, 2024]. Yeh sirf ek chahat nahi hai — yeh ek numbers-backed pattern hai. Ab sawal yeh hai: jab yeh log jate hain, khaas taur par woh log jinhe Pakistan ne khud training di thi — to peeche kya reh jata hai?

## Part 3 — What's Left Behind

Pakistan mein ek doctor banane ka kharcha seedha seedha student se nahi liya jata. Government medical college mein, poori 5-saal ki MBBS degree ki fees taqreeban Rs 1 lakh se 4 lakh tak hoti hai — jabke private college mein yehi degree Rs 80-90 lakh tak par sakti hai [SOURCE: MBBS fee-structure aggregator sites, 2026]. Yeh farq khali fees ka nahi hai — matlab government har seat par apni jaib se bahut zyada kharch karti hai jitna student ada karta hai. Ek PIDE-linked andazay ke mutabiq, ek doctor ko train karne ka asal kharcha, government ke liye, taqreeban $25,000 baithta hai — yani Rs 70 lakh ke aas paas [SOURCE: PIDE-linked figure via Profit by Pakistan Today, 2025] [VERIFY: is andazay ka method public nahi kiya gaya].

Isi andazay ne yeh bhi kaha ke agar 10,000 doctors har saal Pakistan chorte hain, to sirf training ka nuksan hi $250 million salana ban jata hai [SOURCE: PIDE-linked figure via Profit by Pakistan Today, 2025] [VERIFY]. Lekin yahan ek cheez match nahi karti: BEOE ka apna register 2025 mein sirf 3,795 doctors, aur 2024 mein 3,642 doctors dikhata hai — yani jo andaza istemal ho raha hai, woh BEOE ke apne number se ढाई guna zyada hai [SOURCE: TechJuice, 2026]. Ho sakta hai asal number in dono ke beech kahin ho, kyunki BEOE ka register — jaisa Part 2 mein dekha — student aur immigrant visa par jaane walon ko count nahi karta. Lekin jab tak yeh farq reconcile nahi hota, "$250 million" ya "10,000 doctors" ko pakka fact maan kar peesh nahi kiya ja sakta — yeh ek estimate hai jo apni hi assumption par khara hai.

Iska asar sirf paison mein nahi hai. Pakistan mein abhi 319,000 doctors registered hain [SOURCE: Economic Survey 2024-25, via Express Tribune, 2025], jo mulk ki 24 crore se zyada aabadi ke hisaab se ek doctor per hazar log ke qareeb baithta hai — jabke WHO ki sifarish hai teen se chaar doctors per hazar [SOURCE: secondary reporting citing Pakistan Medical Commission, 2025] [VERIFY]. Shehron se bahar yeh tasveer aur kharab hai: kuch rural ilaqon mein yeh ratio ek doctor per 3,500 logon tak gir jata hai, aur kahin kahin ek doctor per 12,000 logon tak [SOURCE: secondary reporting citing Pakistan Medical Commission, 2023] [VERIFY]. Yeh koi naya masla nahi hai — 2016 ke OECD data ke mutabiq, Pakistan pehle se hi America aur Britain mein videshi doctors ka doosra sabse bara source mulk tha, India ke baad [SOURCE: OECD 2016 data, via riazhaq.com] [VERIFY: yeh dahai purana data hai, sirf yeh dikhane ke liye ke yeh silsila naya nahi].

Ek aur PIDE se juri study ne is poore masle ko aur bara paimana diya: unke hisaab se, 2023 mein akele, Pakistan ko human capital ke bahar jaane se $303.4 billion ka productivity loss hua — yeh number migrants ki global GDP mein contribution mein se remittances minus kar ke nikala gaya [SOURCE: PIDE (Khan and Ahmad, 2024), as cited in PIDE, 2024]. Yeh number itna bara hai — Pakistan ki poori annual GDP ke qareeb qareeb 80 percent — ke isay ehtiyat se samajhna zaroori hai: yeh shayad sirf 2023 mein jaane walon ka nuksan nahi, balke bahar baithi poori overseas Pakistani population ki ek saal ki productivity capture karta hai [VERIFY: iska poora method seedha nahi dekha ja saka]. Matlab yeh number training-cost wale $250 million se bilkul alag cheez naap raha hai — dono ko ek doosray mein mila kar nahi dekhna chahiye.

## Part 4 — The Remittance Defense, Tested

Toh ab wapas us dalil par aate hain jis se yeh video shuru hua: "haan log ja rahay hain, lekin remittances toh aa rahi hain." Number khud dekhte hain. FY2025-26 mein Pakistan ko $41.6 billion remittances mile, jo pichle saal ke $38.3 billion se 8.6 percent zyada hai [SOURCE: Business Recorder, 2026]. Sabse zyada paisa kahan se aaya? Saudi Arabia se $9.78 billion, UAE se $8.81 billion, UK se $6.33 billion, aur European mulkon se milakar $5.23 billion [SOURCE: Business Recorder/Gulf News, 2026].

Ab yahan asal cheez samajhne wali hai. 2023 mein, Saudi Arabia, UAE, aur Qatar — teenon milkar — Pakistan ke total emigrants ka 82 percent le gaye, aur usi saal Pakistan ki total remittances ka taqreeban 46 percent bhi inhi teen mulkon se aaya [SOURCE: Gulf Research Center/KAS policy brief, 2025]. Yaad rahay, Part 2 mein humne dekha tha ke inhi Gulf mulkon jaane wale logon mein "highly skilled" aur "highly qualified" ka hissa kabhi 10 percent se zyada nahi raha. Matlab jo bara remittance number Pakistan ki economy ko sambhalta hai, woh mostly unn logon se aa raha hai jo mazdoori, driving, ya construction ka kaam karte hain — na ke unn doctors aur engineers se jinke baray mein "brain drain" ki poori behas hoti hai. 2024 ke apne register mein, "highly skilled" aur "highly qualified" milakar sirf 49,021 log thay, jabke unskilled category akele 366,092 [SOURCE: PPA migration-data summary, citing BEOE, 2026]. Yeh do bilkul alag groups hain — aur "remittances brain drain ko barabar kar deti hain" wali dalil in dono ko ek hi cheez samajh leti hai.

Is dalil ka ek imandar jawab bhi hai, jise nazar-andaz nahi karna chahiye. Ek report ke mutabiq, UK mein settled Pakistani professionals fi-insaan Gulf ke average worker se zyada paisa bhejte hain, chahe unki total tadaad kam ho [SOURCE: Express Tribune, undated 2025-2026] [VERIFY: is article ko seedha access nahi kiya ja saka, sirf iski summary mili]. Agar yeh baat sach nikalti hai, to iska matlab yeh nahi ke skilled emigrants "paisa nahi bhejte" — iska matlab sirf itna ho sakta hai ke unki tadaad Gulf workers ke muqable mein bohot kam hai, is liye unka total remittance share kam dikhta hai, chahe har fard zyada bhej raha ho. Yeh farq zaroori hai: "kam bhejte hain fi-insaan" aur "kam hain tadaad mein" do bilkul alag baatein hain, aur available data se yeh tay nahi hota ke in mein se kaun si sahi hai [VERIFY].

## Part 5 — The Verdict

Toh jawab kya hai us sawal ka jis se yeh video shuru hua tha? Jab ek subsidized doctor ya engineer Pakistan chor deta hai, mulk teen cheezein khota hai jo remittances se pura nahi hoti: training mein lagaya gaya paisa, jiska sahi hisaab abhi tak public taur par kisi ne saaf nahi kiya; workforce ki woh capacity jo pehle se hi kam hai, khaas taur par rural ilaqon mein; aur woh tax jo yeh log Pakistan mein kama kar dete, agar rukte. In teenon ka koi bhi hissa Gulf se aane wali remittances se seedha nahi bharta, kyunki woh paisa ek bilkul alag group of people bhej raha hai.

Yeh video kisi ko yeh nahi keh raha ke log jaana band kar dein, ya ke remittances buri cheez hain — remittances Pakistan ki economy ka aik zaroori hissa hain, aur rahenge. Lekin agar policy banane wale yeh soch kar chain se baithay hain ke "remittances brain drain ka hisaab barabar kar deti hain," to yeh andaza galat buniyad par khara hai — kyunki jo paisa aata hai, aur jo talent jata hai, yeh dono alag raston se, alag logon ke zariye chalte hain. Jab tak yeh farq samjha nahi jata, na training ka nuksan sahi track hoga, na woh policy banegi jo asal masle ko target kare — chahe woh medical seats ki planning ho, ya rural healthcare mein invest karna, ya bas imandari se yeh maan lena ke abhi tak iska poora hisaab kisi ne nahi lagaya.
