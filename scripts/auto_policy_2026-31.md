## Part 1 — The Anomaly

July mein, Toyota Corolla Cross hybrid ki price achanak Rs 13 lakh 60 hazar zyada ho gayi — same engine, same badge, sirf price tag bara ho gaya [SOURCE: Bloom Pakistan, 2026]. Honda ki HR-V hybrid bhi isi jaisi amount mehngi hui [SOURCE: Bloom Pakistan, 2026]. Kisi ne is par vote nahi diya. Parliament mein koi naya tax discuss nahi hua. Toh yeh hua kyun — aur aath hafton baad bhi, government ke paas iska jawab kyun nahi hai?

Asal mein hua yeh: Pakistan ki auto policy — jo car industry ke liye tax aur rules tay karti hai — 30 June ko khatam ho gayi [SOURCE: ProPakistani, 2026]. Uski jagah lene ke liye kuch bhi tayyar nahi tha. Hybrid cars par saalon se ek discounted tax rate lagta tha. Jaise hi purani policy khatam hui, woh discount bhi khatam ho gaya. Har nayi hybrid par tax seedha 25 percent GST — yani sales tax — par wapis chala gaya, jitna baaqi sab cars par lagta hai [SOURCE: Dawn Business, 2026]. Kuch dealers ne toh kuch dinon ke liye hybrid orders lena hi band kar diya [SOURCE: Profit by Pakistan Today, 2026]. Woh dekhna chahte thay ke kahin government apna faisla wapis na le.

Nahi liya. Hafton baad bhi nahi liya. Ek nayi policy draft mein maujood hai — lekin August ki shuruaat mein, government ne khud apni yeh draft policy chupke se rok di, kyunki manufacturers ne warning di ke yeh policy electric vehicles ki taraf itna jhuk gayi hai ke Pakistan sirf bahar se aayi hui EV kits jorne wali assembly line ban kar reh jayega [SOURCE: Pakbiz, 2026] [SOURCE: TechJuice, 2026]. Ek nayi committee banayi gayi. Ab tak ki khabron ke mutabiq, us committee ne sirf "in-principle" manzoori di hai — policy abhi tak federal cabinet tak nahi pohanchi [SOURCE: ProPakistani, 2026] [VERIFY: recording se pehle current status confirm karein, mid-August tak situation tezi se badal rahi thi].

Government ek document mukammal nahi kar pa rahi, aur is dauran, is gap ka kharcha khareedne wale uthate hain. Toh car tariffs ki yeh larai us price tag se kya taluq rakhti hai jo abhi aap showroom mein dekh rahe hain?

## Part 2 — The Mechanism

Pehle yeh samajhte hain ke yeh gayab policy asal mein karti kya hai. Har paanch saal baad, government car industry ke liye do numbers tay karti hai: import hone wali car ya part par kitna tax lagega, aur Pakistan mein banti hui car ka kitna hissa waqai Pakistan mein banaya jana chahiye.

Jab koi cheez bahar se Pakistan mein aati hai, uspar ek tax lagta hai — usay tariff kehte hain. Zeer-e-ghor draft policy is tariff ko kaafi kam karna chahti hai — abhi har 100 rupay ki value par taqreeban 11 rupay tariff lagta hai, ise 2030 tak ghata kar taqreeban 7 rupay tak lana hai, aur uske baad iska target aur bhi neeche hai [SOURCE: Profit by Pakistan Today, 2026]. Ek poori tarah import ki hui, chalane ke liye tayyar car par zyada se zyada 15 percent tax lagega, bas [SOURCE: PakWheels Blog, 2026] [VERIFY: draft-stage, finalize nahi hua]. Iske badle mein, draft pehli baar localization ke liye asal numbers maangti hai — localization ka matlab hai ke car ka kitna hissa Pakistan mein hi banaya jaye: 2030 tak, chhoti gari ya motorbike ki har 100 rupay ki value mein se 85 rupay Pakistan mein hi bane hone chahiye; chhoti cars ke liye, 2031 tak yeh 80 se 90 rupay tak barh jata hai [SOURCE: PakWheels Blog, 2026] [VERIFY: draft-stage]. Electric vehicles ke liye khaas taur par bane parts ko sabse pehle riayat milti hai — pehle teen saal sirf 1 percent customs duty, chauthe saal 5 percent tak barh jata hai [SOURCE: PakWheels Blog, 2026] [VERIFY: draft-stage]. Yeh utna lamba waqt nahi hai jitna sunne mein lagta hai: sirf chaar saal ki asli protection, uske baad EV parts ka business bhi usi competition ka samna karega jo baaqi sab ko karna parta hai.

Yeh hai woh deal jo government banana chahti hai: jaldi kam prices, iske badle is waade ke ke local manufacturing waqai us gap ko bhar degi jo peeche reh jayega. Kaghaz par yeh theek deal lagti hai. Amal mein yeh is liye toot gayi kyunki government ke andar hi koi is baat par muttafiq nahi tha ke kitni tezi se aage barhna hai. Ministry of Industries chahti thi ke tariff cuts ko naram kiya jaye — iske saath ek naya excise duty, yani ek aur qisam ka tax, laga kar, taake asal protection level zyada na badle, chahe headline tariff number kam hi kyun na ho jaye [SOURCE: PakWheels Blog, 2026] [VERIFY]. Ministry of Commerce apni baat par qaim rahi: yeh cut waqt par hona chahiye, kyunki Pakistan ne poori economy mein tariffs kam karne ka wada kiya hai, sirf cars mein nahi [SOURCE: PakWheels Blog, 2026] [VERIFY]. IMF, bahar se yeh sab dekh raha tha, usne ek proposed rasta rad kar diya — electric vehicles ke liye khaas 1 percent sales tax — aur us standard 18 percent rate par qaim raha jo baaqi sab dete hain [SOURCE: PakWheels Blog, 2026] [VERIFY: secondhand report, IMF ka original document seedha access nahi kiya gaya].

Teen parties, teen positions, aur ek policy jo unki larai ke dauran khatam ho gayi. Yeh pehli baar nahi hai ke Pakistan ki government ne is line par chalne ki koshish ki ho — local manufacturers ko itna hi protect karo ke woh waqai compete kar sakein, itna nahi ke unhe kabhi karna hi na pare. Dekhte hain pichli do baar jab yeh koshish hui to kya hua.

## Part 3 — The History

2016 mein, government ne is draft ki tarah koi percentage target set nahi kiya tha. Iske bajaye, usne sirf import karna local banane se mehnga bana diya: Category-A manufacturer bahar se mangwaye gaye parts par 10 percent customs duty deta tha, aur Pakistan mein bane parts par 25 percent — ulta lagta hai, lekin jaan boojh kar, taake paanch saal tak local sourcing hi sasta option rahe [SOURCE: Board of Investment, Pakistan, primary policy document]. Kaghaz par, yeh kaafi kaamyab raha: 12 se zyada nayi manufacturers ne Pakistan mein aane ka elaan kiya [SOURCE: industry analysis, 2026] [VERIFY: yeh figure industry commentary se hai, koi official ginti nahi]. Amal mein, un mein se zyada tar plans chupke se rok diye gaye ya kabhi shuru hi nahi hue, kyunki rupay ki value girti rahi aur rules sab ke paon tale badalte rahe [SOURCE: industry analysis, 2026] [VERIFY].

Agli policy, 2021 se 2026 tak, ne bilkul alag tareeqa apnaya: import duties ki jagah export targets. Result bilkul aadha-aadha raha. Motorcycles aur tractors ne apna target bahut peeche chor diya — apne target se taqreeban dedh guna zyada export kiya [SOURCE: AutoWorld Journal, 2026] [VERIFY: primary EDB/PAMA export data seedha confirm nahi hua]. Cars ne nahi kiya. Exported cars us dauran Pakistan mein bani hui total gadiyon ka 1 percent se bhi kam rahin — jo policy ne wada kiya tha uske aas paas bhi nahi [SOURCE: AutoWorld Journal, 2026] [VERIFY].

Toh yeh tariffs aur timelines wali larai koi nayi baat nahi hai, sirf nayi numbers ke saath. Yeh ek hi sawaal ka teesra try hai, har baar alag tareeqe ke saath, aur pichli dono koshishein aisi car industry nahi bana sakin jo protection ke baghair khud khadi ho sake. Yehi wajah hai ke abhi ka yeh gridlock jitna dikhta hai usse zyada important hai — aur yehi wajah hai ke is draft par larne wale logon ke is baar risk kaun uthaye, iske bilkul alag khayalat hain.

## Part 4 — The Stakes

Parts manufacturers ki association, PAAPAM, kehti hai ke government jo hisaab laga rahi hai woh chalega hi nahi. PAAPAM ke mutabiq, unke members doosre mumalik ke manufacturers ke muqable mein 34 percent zyada cost bardaasht karte hain, kyunki Pakistan gari banane ke liye zaroori raw materials mein se ek bhi khud nahi banata — na auto-grade steel, na plastic resin, na khaas rubber [SOURCE: Business Recorder, 2026]. Yeh sab bahar se aata hai, aur uske upar mehngi bijli, zyada taxes, aur mehnga qarza bhi hai. Isi wajah se woh chahte hain ke government import duties 50 se 60 percent par rakhe, aur local parts par kam az kam 45 percent protection ki guarantee de — jo abhi ke draft se taqreeban teen guna zyada hai [SOURCE: Business Recorder, 2026].

PAAPAM apni warning ke saath asal numbers bhi deti hai: unke hisaab se 10 lakh se zyada logon ki rozi-roti is se juri hui hai — 3 lakh se zyada log seedha is industry mein kaam karte hain, aur 7 lakh aur log is par indirectly depend karte hain [SOURCE: Profit by Pakistan Today, 2026] [VERIFY: yeh PAAPAM ka apna andaza hai, koi independent labour count nahi]. Agar government apne proposed timeline par tariffs kam kar deti hai, unka kehna hai ke industry adjust nahi karegi — khatam ho jayegi.

Is dalil ko sunna zaroori hai. Lekin iska ek jawab bhi utna hi zaroori hai, kyunki yeh dalil maan kar chalti hai ke maqsad sirf abhi maujood manufacturers ko bachana hai. Ek mazboot dalil yeh bhi hai ke protection hi woh cheez hai jisne Pakistan ki parts industry ko kabhi competitive banne hi nahi diya. Do policies aur taqreeban ek dahai ki tariff protection ke baad bhi, wohi local supply base nahi ban saka jo dono policies banana chahti thin. Ek waqt ke baad, wohi purani sharton par mazeed waqt dena ek mauqa kam aur kabhi na uran sakne ka bahana zyada lagta hai. Kisi ke paas bhi itna saaf saboot nahi ke faisla kar sake yeh baat sach hai kaun si — yehi cheez isay ek asal ikhtilaf banati hai, na ke ek hal shuda masla jise government maanne se inkar kar rahi hai. Yeh larai kaun jeetega, iska faisla abhi bhi ho raha hai — ek aisi committee room mein jo koi car khareedne wala kabhi nahi dekhega.

## Part 5 — The Verdict

Ab tak ki khabron ke mutabiq, ek nayi draft ne ek internal rukawat paar kar li hai — Dar committee se "in-principle" manzoori mil gayi hai — aur yeh abhi bhi Ministry of Industries aur uske baad federal cabinet ki taraf apna raasta bana rahi hai [SOURCE: ProPakistani, 2026] [VERIFY: recording se pehle current status confirm karein]. Is process se bahar koi nahi janta ke final version PAAPAM ki dheemi timeline ki taraf jhukega ya us tariff schedule ki taraf jo Commerce aur IMF chahte hain.

Is baar farq sirf itna hai ke intezar ka kharcha ab sirf ek farz nahi raha. Yeh July mein hi ek price tag par nazar aa chuka hai, mahinon pehle jab Islamabad mein kisi ko koi pakka faisla lena tha hi nahi. Agar aakhri policy — jab bhi cabinet se pass ho — 2016 aur 2021 wala hi "pehle protection" wala tareeqa dohrati hai, to imandari se yehi umeed rakhni chahiye: kuch waqt ke liye bachi hui industry, prices jo waade ke mutabiq neeche nahi aatin, aur ek aur paanch saal ka waqt jo 2031 mein yehi behas dobara shuru kar dega. Jo log abhi Pakistan mein gari khareed rahe hain unke liye, jo price aap abhi dekh rahe hain woh woh price nahi hai jise policy theek karne ki koshish kar rahi hai — yeh us policy ki price hai jo abhi tak bani hi nahi.
